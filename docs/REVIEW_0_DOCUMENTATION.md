## 2. Community & Context
* **Context:** In inclusive classrooms, children with developmental challenges (such as ADHD, Autism Spectrum Disorder, Dyslexia, and Mild Intellectual Disabilities) learn alongside neurotypical children.
* **Why Technical Support is Needed:** Traditional textbook-and-blackboard teaching methods are rigid and text-heavy. Teachers currently lack an integrated digital platform that adapts standard curriculum topics into visual, sensory-calibrated formats suitable for whole-class projection.

---

## 3. Problem Statement & Evidences
* **Problem Statement:** Children with developmental disabilities and learning difficulties often face barriers to accessing engaging, accessible, and individually appropriate educational resources in classroom environments.
* **Evidence Points:**
  1. **50%:** Inaccessibility of specialized learning materials in mainstream inclusive classrooms.
  2. **40%:** Proportion of educational institutions lacking dedicated teacher assistive training.
  3. **42%:** Risk increase of failing foundational literacy and numeracy benchmarks among neurodiverse learners without multimodal aids.

---

## 4. Specific Objectives
1. **Develop a teacher-led platform** providing curriculum-aligned learning resources for inclusive classrooms.
2. **Deliver engaging videos, interactive lessons, and educational games** through classroom projection.
3. **Support diverse learning needs** using accessible visuals, audio instructions, and simplified explanations.
4. **Improve whole-class participation** through interactive activities designed for collaborative classroom learning.

---

## 5. Existing Solutions & Gap Analysis
* **DIKSHA:** National digital repository with extensive textbook contents, but lacks condition-specific sensory calibrations or whole-class collaborative game flows.
* **ePathshala:** Offers digital textbooks and static audio, but lacks interactive tactile/visual activities for attention retention.
* **YouTube:** Offers videos, but introduces ads, clutter, distraction, and lacks systematic curriculum-to-disability adaptation.
* **EduBridge's Gap Filled:** A teacher-operated classroom projection hub combining CBSE curriculum alignment, disorder-specific adaptation settings, and distraction-free interactive games.

---

## 6. Proposed Methodology & Architecture
* **Stage 1 (Requirements):** Identify teacher needs, syllabus topics (CBSE Grades 1–5), and accessibility parameters.
* **Stage 2 (Design):** Design clean UI/UX with high contrast, dyslexia fonts, large touch targets, and presentation mode.
* **Stage 3 (Build):** Implement modular web interface with visual lessons, step-by-step counting/matching, and group quiz games.
* **Stage 4 (Deploy):** Classroom demonstration and teacher feedback evaluation.

---

## 7. Stakeholder Survey Plan
* **Target Stakeholders:** Special educators, primary school teachers, and parents of neurodiverse children.
* **Data Collection Method:** Semi-structured questionnaires and direct classroom observation sessions focusing on attention spans during digital vs. textbook learning.
